heriTrac

A family tree that works out what everyone is to you, instead of asking you to type it in.

Open heriTrac Download the single file
01 / What it is

You record who is related to whom. It works out the rest.

Add a parent, a partner, a child or a sibling, and every other word follows from the shape of the family. Pick anyone as the viewpoint and the whole tree relabels itself around them: your grandmother's brother becomes your great uncle, and from your cousin's side he is a grandfather.

Only the links are stored. No relationship is ever typed in, so none of them can disagree with each other.

02 / What it keeps

Nothing.

No account
There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to.
No storage
The family lives in the browser tab and leaves as a file you keep. Close the tab and it is gone.
No tracking
No analytics, no cookies, no third party scripts. The page asks the network for two fonts and nothing else.
03 / What is coming

Shareable links, as an option.

A link you can send to a relative so they can look at the family, and sign in if they want to help build it. Working entirely offline stays the default, because that is the part worth protecting.

04 / Tell me it is wrong

Especially if a word for a relative is off.

Kinship words differ between languages and between families. If heriTrac calls somebody the wrong thing, that is the most useful thing you can report.
